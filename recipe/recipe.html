Easy Banana Bread Recipe


How to Make 1 Hour Ready Banana Bread

Ingredients

  • 3 Over-ripe bananas (a peel with lots of browning)
  • 1/3 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 2 eggs (beaten)
  • 1 1/2 cups of all-purpose flour
  • 2/3 cup of sugar
  • 1 tsp. of vanilla
  • 1 tsp. of baking powder
  • 1 tsp. of salt

Steps

  1. Preheat Oven to 300°F for 10 minutes
  2. Grease loaf pan
  3. Add bananas to mixing bowl and mash together with a fork
  4. In the same bowl stir in the butter,sugar, egg and vanilla
  5. Add flour and stir until there are no streaks of flour left
  6. Transfer to pan, scrape sides of mixing bowl to get all the batter
  7. Leave to bake for 45-50 minutes!!!
  8. Enjoy!
